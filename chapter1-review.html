The trap most people fall into

Factors go into a number, so they're small: the factors of 12 are 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 12.

Multiples come out of a number, so they're big: the multiples of 12 are 12, 24, 36, 48…

Say each answer out loud, in your own words, before you open it. If you can explain it, you know it.
