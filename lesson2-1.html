Lesson 2.1: Rational numbers

Fractions and decimals, positive and negative. Where they sit on a number line, and how to write them in simplest form.

The trap on the negative side

{3/4} is bigger than {1/2}. But −{3/4} is smaller than −{1/2}, because it's farther left of zero.

On the negative side, the farther from zero, the smaller the number.

Say each answer out loud, in your own words, before you open it. If you can explain it, you know it.
